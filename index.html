Case 31: Employees Federal Credit Union

IC11-2 | R1, R2, R5, and R6

Case Background and Objectives

  • Employees Federal Credit Union (EFCU) commissioned a member survey to investigate borrowing decisions, service awareness, and operational performance.
  • The case describes a random sample of 300 members; 128 usable cases appear in the supplied cleaned SPSS dataset.
  • Analyses use available valid responses for each question; two-sided tests use α = .05.

R5 — Interpretation

  • Regular share accounts have the highest reported use (83/128 = 64.8%).
  • Signature loans and new-car loans are known but rarely used (104 and 99 aware nonusers, respectively).
  • Line-of-credit loans show no reported use and 48/128 (37.5%) unaware respondents.
  • All 14 equal-proportion goodness-of-fit tests reject a uniform distribution (p < .001); this does not test overall awareness against a benchmark.

Conclusions and Recommendations

  • R1: Direct joining motivations cannot be established without Q2.
  • R2: The available rate-perception measures are near neutral; the direct borrowing-reason question Q29 is missing.
  • R5: Awareness is uneven, and several loan products are rarely used; target communication toward less familiar services.
  • R6: Members generally report favorable operational perceptions, while privacy confidence has room to improve.
  • Results describe the respondents and should not be interpreted as causal evidence.
